# neinainoi website

This file is the standing brief for this repository. Read it fully before writing code.
Decisions marked DECIDED are fixed. Items marked OPEN must be asked about, not guessed.

## 1. What neinainoi is

neinainoi is a brand of wearable architecture. It sells three reconfigurable fabric parts
that the wearer joins with connectors to build garments, harnesses and body structures.
The pieces are worn on their own or over other clothes.

- Descriptor (DECIDED): "wearable architecture you build yourself."
- Short signature (DECIDED): "build yourself." Used on a card inside the packaging.
- Character: severe and precise as an object, playful in use, can be kinky. References are
  Rick Owens and Matieres Fecales. Never cute, never streetwear.
- It is a brand, not a project. The voice is "we", meaning the team of neinainoi.
  The founder is not named or shown unless she adds herself.
- Price target: 800 to 1,500 EUR. The object is premium; the voice is dry and short.
- Audience is wide in age (10 to 80). The site may be strange, but nobody may get stuck.

## 2. The three parts

| Name | Old working name | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| nei | Band | straight bar |
| nai | Bend | arch |
| noi | Bone | wishbone, a stem that splits into two legs |

- DECIDED: this mapping is confirmed by the founder.
- The collective noun is always "parts". Never "modules", "units" or "pieces" in copy.
- Parts carry snap connection points along their length at a fixed pitch.

### Geometry rules

- Every dimension resolves to one base unit: `w = 43.671 mm`, the width of a limb.
- Height of nei and noi is `6w`. The nai arch has its centreline apex at half that height.
- Snap pitch equals `w`. Snaps are inset `w/2` from each end.
- The real outlines exist as locked vector paths in a PDF held by the founder. Those paths
  are the single source of truth. Never redraw or approximate them by eye.
- Until that PDF is in `/assets/source/`, use the stand-in paths below and keep them in
  one file so they can be swapped in a single commit.

Stand-in centrelines, in units of `w`, y pointing down, stroke width 1, butt caps:

```
nei  M0 6 V0
nai  M-1.5 6 C-1.5 4.1 -0.9 3 0 3 C0.9 3 1.5 4.1 1.5 6
noi  M-1.5 6 C-1.5 3.8 0 2.6 0 0 M1.5 6 C1.5 3.8 0 2.6 0 0
```

## 3. Identity

### Logo (DECIDED for paper card and hangtag)

The mark is called Idol: one nai as a head, one nei laid horizontally as shoulders, one noi
as a body. It uses exactly one of each part. The approved artwork is on
`assets/source/boards/Recommended_lockup.pdf`: nai (neon) stands on top of nei, nei shows
three snap points, noi hangs below. The board files are flat JPEGs, not vectors. Never
retrace them. The vector master is still needed before the Idol is used on the site.
The Idol does not appear on any public page before launch.

Primary lockup, measured on the board: Idol left, wordmark right. Wordmark width is 1.47 x
the Idol height; wordmark ink top sits 0.21 x Idol height below the Idol top; the gap is
0.21 x Idol height.

- Line thickness of the mark is never changed. To make it lighter, make it smaller.
- When the wordmark sits under the mark, it is sized to exactly the mark's width.

### Wordmark (DECIDED)

- "neinainoi", one word, always lowercase.
- Typeface: Sofia Sans Extra Condensed, weight 900.
- On dark or light grounds the middle syllable "nai" may be neon. On a neon ground the
  whole word is one ink.

### Case (DECIDED)

- The whole brand is lowercase, headlines included. neinainoi, nei, nai and noi are
  lowercase everywhere, in every typeface, no exceptions.
- Full sentences may start with a capital only in long running text such as terms.

### It must not look machine made (DECIDED, strict)

The founder does not want anything that reads as generated by an AI following default
design habits. Treat the following as banned unless she asks for them:

- Rows or grids of equal rounded cards. Tiles with a small caption above and below.
- Small uppercase monospace labels used as decoration, section numbers like 01 / 02.
- Gradients, glows, glass effects, soft shadows, pill buttons, emoji, stock icons.
- Centred hero with headline, subline and two buttons. Any landing page pattern.
- Explanatory microcopy. If something needs a sentence to explain it, redesign it.

What makes it hers instead: the real part outlines, the snap grid as the only layout
system, her own photographs and footage shown large and uncropped, and her own drawing
hand where illustration is needed. When unsure, remove rather than add.

### Type

| Role | Face | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark, headlines | Sofia Sans Extra Condensed | 900 |
| Descriptive lines, body | Sofia Sans Extra Condensed | 500 |
| Part names, specs, prices, labels | Martian Mono | 400 |

- DECIDED: Instrument Serif is removed everywhere, including the web. The Type and colour
  board still shows it; the brief wins.
- Any repeated instance of the wordmark in one view uses one size, one weight and one
  letter spacing.

### Colour (DECIDED)

| Token | Hex | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| pitch | #0B0A0C | ground | about 68% |
| tar | #1B1A1D | raised surface | about 8% |
| ash | #8C8780 | spec text, hardware | about 6% |
| chalk | #EDEBE6 | parts, text | about 16% |
| neon | #9500FF | one accent | about 2% |

- Neon is exactly #9500FF. It must never drift toward pink or magenta.
- Neon marks one thing per composition. By default that thing is nai.
- Printed paper (hangtag, packaging card) is the exception: a full neon ground with
  pitch as the only ink. No white on neon.
- Do not set small text in neon on pitch. The contrast is about 3.4 to 1.

### Writing rules

- No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere, in copy or in UI.
- Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No marketing adjectives.

## 4. The website

### Concept (DECIDED in direction)

The site behaves like the product. It is not an editorial scroll and not a portfolio grid.
The home page is a field of snap points on the pitch grid. Visitors drag nei, nai and noi
onto it and snap them together. Photographs, looks and text also hang from snap points.
One simple rule that becomes complex through use.

It should be the most unusual fashion site the visitor has seen: interactive, odd, and in
places not immediately obvious. Two things make that safe:

1. A plain text index is always one action away from any state. It lists looks, parts,
   prices, shipping and contact as ordinary links.
2. Cart and checkout are completely conventional.

### Sections

1. **Field (home).** The snap grid with loose parts. Building is the navigation.
2. **Looks.** Named configurations made by neinainoi. Each has a name, photographs, a
   recipe (for example 4 nei, 12 nai, 7 noi) and a price. A look is the main thing sold.
3. **Builder.** The visitor makes their own look on the grid, sees the live part count
   and price, and buys exactly that. This is the Field with a cart attached.
4. **Parts.** More parts, sold only in kits. DECIDED for now: a kit is a set of all three
   parts in equal numbers, in sizes 4, 8, 12, 20 and 40 (a kit of 8 is 8 nei + 8 nai + 8 noi).
   No single parts. Kits by part type may come later, so keep the kit definition in data.
5. **Worn.** Photos and videos from Instagram and TikTok posts that tag neinainoi,
   selected by the team before they appear. No public upload feature.
6. **Index.** The plain text view described above.
7. **Later, not now:** accessories and collectibles by artists and architects that attach
   to snap points. Design the data model so a snap point can hold a third kind of object.

### Photography

- Shown as a numbered series that grows over time, not as seasonal campaigns.
- Backstage and work in progress footage is part of the series, not a separate area.

### What may be shown, and when (DECIDED)

The founder is not filing a design patent now. Her protection is timing: the system is
not shown until a press launch (design media such as designboom) that sends traffic to
the shop. Until she declares the launch:

- Public pages may show finished looks on a body. They may not show single parts laid
  flat, the connector in close up, assembly steps, or how the system works.
- The Field, the Builder and the Parts section therefore stay unpublished. Build them on
  a private preview URL only.
- Phase 0 is the only public page: a holding page at neinainoi.com with the wordmark,
  the descriptor, one cleared image and an email signup. Build this first.
- Phase 1 goes live on launch day, with the shop open.

### Location (DECIDED)

Brand copy never mentions where the company is registered, based or manufactured, and
never names a city or country as origin.

- DEFERRED: a shop selling to consumers in the EU must identify the seller and address in a
  legal notice. No entity or lawyer yet. Not needed before phase 1. Do not write it until the
  founder supplies the exact text. A registered seller is a launch dependency for Shopify.

## 5. Technical direction

- Hosting is static: Cloudflare Pages or Netlify. The domain is at Porkbun.
- Prefer a small stack: Vite, TypeScript, SVG for parts and the grid. Add a framework
  only when a concrete need appears, and say why.
- Parts are data. One definition per part (path, snap points, name, price) drives the
  Field, the Builder, look recipes and the cart.
- A look is a list of placed parts (part, position, rotation, which snaps are joined). The
  same structure serialises to a URL so any build can be shared and reopened.
- Snapping is geometric: two snap points join when they coincide on the pitch grid.
- Must work with touch, mouse and keyboard. Must work on a phone.
- Respect reduced motion. Every image has alt text. The Index works without JavaScript.
- Commerce (DECIDED): Shopify used headless. This site is the front; Shopify handles cart,
  checkout, tax and orders. Integrate in phase 1, not before.
- Currency: EUR only. Shipping: Europe and UK. Language: English only.
- Prices shown to consumers include VAT and import duty (EU and UK law require tax-inclusive
  display). Shipping is paid by the customer and added at checkout. Goods ship from China.
  Rates are placeholders until a customs broker confirms the duty code.
- Email signup: Buttondown, account hello@neinainoi.com, double opt-in. The list moves to
  Shopify at launch.
- Hosting: Cloudflare Pages. The private preview sits behind Cloudflare Access.
- Screen size of w: 24 px at phone width, growing with the viewport (`--w` in CSS).
- The manufacturing process is not decided. Do not write copy about technology, knitting
  or waste until the founder supplies it. No general environmental claims at any time.

## 6. How to work in this repository

- Ask before guessing. One clear recommendation, not a menu of options.
- Make small targeted edits. Do not rewrite files that work.
- Verify geometry and text fit by rendering and measuring, never by eye or estimate.
- Request source files (vector, PDF) before reconstructing any asset.
- Never rename a public identifier for a part once it is in use.
